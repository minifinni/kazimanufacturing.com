Why Nepal

0%

0% UK Import Duty

Under the UK Developing Countries Trading Scheme (DCTS), goods from Nepal enter the UK duty-free. This saves you ~12% compared to other Asian manufacturing hubs.

🧵

Skilled Workforce

Nepal has a rich textile heritage and skilled craftspeople. Our workers bring decades of experience in garment manufacturing and embroidery.

💚

Ethical Production

We pay fair wages and maintain safe working conditions. Our factory is audited and certified. When you work with us, you support sustainable manufacturing.
