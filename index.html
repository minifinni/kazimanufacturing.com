/ Why Nepal

The structural
advantage.

🇬🇧
0% UK import duty

Nepal's LDC trade status means zero tariffs into the UK — the same structural advantage Bangladesh used to build its industry. Except we're not racing to the bottom.

🏔️
Skilled workforce

Nepal's garment workers have produced technical outdoor gear for global brands for decades. That precision translates directly to streetwear construction.

✅
Verified ethical wages

Workers paid above national minimum. Verifiable. We'll share the receipts.

📦
Landed cost advantage

£12–18 per hoodie landed in the UK. Chinese equivalent is £9–12 but carries 12–20% import duty. We're cost-competitive before you add the story.
