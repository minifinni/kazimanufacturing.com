What's Included

  • Chest Logos — Left or right chest, up to 12cm wide
  • Back Pieces — Large back embroidery up to 30cm
  • Cap & Beanie Embroidery — Curved or flat bills
  • Sleeve Details — Arm patches, text, logos
  • 3D Puff Embroidery — Raised effect for standout logos
  • Traditional Hand Embroidery — Artisan-crafted details

Technical Specs

  • Up to 15 thread colours per design
  • Tajima and Barudan machines
  • Digitizing service included for bulk orders
  • Minimum stitch count: 5,000 stitches
  • Turnaround: 2-3 weeks for samples, 4-6 weeks bulk
