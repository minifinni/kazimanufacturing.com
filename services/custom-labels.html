What's Included

  • Woven Neck Labels — Main brand labels, damask or satin weave
  • Care Labels — Wash instructions, composition, origin
  • Size Labels — Woven or printed size tabs
  • Flag Labels — Side seam branding
  • Embroidered Patches — Sew-on or iron-on, any shape
  • PVC Patches — Rubber patches for outdoor/workwear
  • Leather Patches — Genuine or PU leather
  • Hang Tags — Cardboard tags with string

Technical Specs

  • Woven labels: 50 denier minimum for clarity
  • Up to 8 colours on woven labels
  • Embroidered patches: merrow or laser border
  • PVC: 2D or 3D moulded options
  • Heat cut or ultrasonic cut available
  • Fold options: centre fold, end fold, mitre fold
