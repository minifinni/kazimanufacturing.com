/ Our Process

How DTG
Works.

01

Artwork Prep

Send high-res PNG (300dpi) or vector file. We check printability and suggest adjustments.

02

Garment Prep

Garments pretreated for ink adhesion, especially important for dark colours.

03

Print & Cure

Direct inkjet printing followed by heat press curing for wash durability.

04

Quality Check

Each garment inspected for print quality, colour accuracy, and finish.
