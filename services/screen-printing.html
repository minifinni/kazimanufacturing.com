What's Included

  • Up to 6 Colour Prints — Multi-colour graphics with precise registration
  • Water-Based Inks — Soft hand feel, eco-friendly, breathable
  • Plastisol Inks — Vibrant colours, durable, opaque on darks
  • Discharge Printing — Soft prints on dark garments
  • Large Format — A2+ prints, all-over designs
  • Specialty Inks — Metallics, puff, glow in dark (on request)

Technical Specs

  • Manual and automatic press capability
  • Up to 6 colours per design
  • Screen setup: one-time fee per colour
  • Print area: up to 40x50cm standard
  • Sample turnaround: 2-3 weeks
  • Bulk turnaround: 3-5 weeks

/ Our Process

How Screen Printing
Works.

01

Artwork & Separations

Vector artwork separated into colour layers. Each colour = one screen.

02

Screen Setup

Screens coated, exposed, and prepared. One-time setup fee per colour.

03

Test Print

Sample printed and approved before bulk production begins.

04

Bulk Production

Full production with flash curing between colours. Final heat cure.
