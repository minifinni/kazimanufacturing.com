Our Story.

Connecting ethical brands with Nepal's skilled garment manufacturers.

Why We Started

KAZI was born from a simple observation: Nepal has incredible garment manufacturing talent, but international brands struggle to find and work with Nepali producers. Meanwhile, Bangladesh and Vietnam dominate the conversation.

We saw an opportunity. Nepal offers the same 0% UK import duty advantage as Bangladesh (both are LDCs), but with a difference — Nepal's industry is smaller, more agile, and deeply rooted in traditional craftsmanship.

Our mission is to make Nepal manufacturing accessible to ethical brands worldwide. We bridge the gap between international buyers and local producers, handling the complexity so you can focus on building your brand.

/ The Industry

Nepal's Garment
Industry.

🧶 Knitwear Heritage

Nepal has a long tradition of knitwear production, particularly in the Kathmandu Valley. The country's knitwear exports have built a reputation for quality craftsmanship and unique designs that blend traditional techniques with modern fashion.

🏔️ Outdoor Expertise

Home to Mount Everest, Nepal's outdoor and trekking gear industry has decades of experience producing technical apparel for extreme conditions. This expertise translates to high-quality construction in all garment types.

🪡 Artisan Craftsmanship

Nepali embroidery and handicraft traditions date back centuries. This artisan heritage means meticulous attention to detail in every stitch — whether traditional hand embroidery or modern machine work.

📈 Growing Export Sector

While smaller than Bangladesh or Vietnam, Nepal's garment export sector is growing. The country is positioning itself as an ethical alternative with competitive costs and unique value propositions.
