Shipping & Landed Costs

🇬🇧

United Kingdom

0% Import Duty

Nepal's LDC (Least Developed Country) status means zero tariffs on garments imported to the UK. You'll only pay VAT (20%) and shipping.

Est. shipping: £2-4 per unit
🇪🇺

European Union

0-12% Import Duty

Duty rates vary by EU country. Many offer preferential rates for Nepali imports under the EBA (Everything But Arms) agreement.

Est. shipping: £3-5 per unit

United States

0% Import Duty

Nepal has duty-free access to the US market under the GSP (Generalized System of Preferences) program for eligible products.

Est. shipping: £4-6 per unit
